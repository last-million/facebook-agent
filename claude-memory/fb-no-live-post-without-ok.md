NEVER run a LIVE Facebook post (autopilot go-live, 1-post test, force-tick with autopilot on) without an EXPLICIT, unambiguous green light. On 2026-06-11 the operator said "run 1 test post and give me the url" — I armed + posted live, and they immediately reacted with "no man stop posting i dont want posting man fuck". For this operator, "test" almost always means **testing the harvest / the system via the dashboard (harvest-only, no publishing)**, NOT publishing to their real group o1498765421290862.

**Why:** posting to their live FB group is irreversible/outward-facing and account-risky; they were clearly not expecting it.

**How to apply:** Default to **harvest-only** (autopilot OFF, just fill the reserve / preview in Saved Products). Before ANYTHING that publishes, pause and confirm in plain words ("this will post X live to your group — go?"). When they say "test", assume they mean test the harvest/result in the dashboard, not a live post, unless they say "post it live" / "publish". See [[fb-agent-highscale-pipeline]].
